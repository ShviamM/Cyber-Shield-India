NETRAKSH V2 – INDIA'S DIGITAL BODYGUARD

MISSION

Redesign Netraksh for ordinary Indian citizens, including senior citizens, homemakers, students, small business owners, and first-time smartphone users.

The app must be usable by someone with little or no cybersecurity knowledge.

The design philosophy is:

"Less typing. More protection."

COMPETITOR POSITIONING

Do NOT copy Truecaller.

Truecaller solves:

* Caller Identification
* Spam Calling

Netraksh solves:

* Scam Calls
* Scam SMS
* WhatsApp Scams
* Fraud Links
* QR Code Fraud
* UPI Fraud
* Online Shopping Fraud
* Investment Scams
* Job Scams
* Family Digital Protection

Position Netraksh as:

"India's Digital Bodyguard"

HOME SCREEN REDESIGN

The home screen must have only 4 primary actions visible above the fold.

1. Check Number
2. Check Link
3. Check Message
4. Report Fraud

Each action must be represented using:

* Large icon
* Large text
* One tap access

No complex menus.

No technical dashboards.

No cybersecurity terminology.

QR SCANNER

Add a floating action button.

"Scan Before You Pay"

One tap opens camera.

Automatically scan:

* QR Codes
* UPI QR
* Payment QR
* Suspicious QR

Display:

✅ Safe

⚠ Be Careful

🚨 Dangerous

VOICE-FIRST EXPERIENCE

Add voice input everywhere.

Users should be able to say:

"Check this number"

"Is this message safe?"

"Can I trust this QR code?"

Support:

* Hindi
* Hinglish
* English

WHATSAPP INTEGRATION

Implement Android and iOS share extensions.

Allow users to:

Share WhatsApp message
Share SMS
Share Screenshot
Share Link

Directly into Netraksh.

The app should analyze content automatically.

RISK LANGUAGE

Never show:

* Threat score
* IOC
* Phishing
* Malware
* Domain reputation

Instead show:

✅ Safe

⚠ Be Careful

🚨 Dangerous

And explain in plain language.

Example:

"This message asks for money urgently. Please verify before sending money."

HINDI-FIRST DESIGN

On first launch ask:

Preferred Language

* हिन्दी
* English

All important screens must support Hindi.

FAMILY PROTECTION

Prominently display:

"Protect Your Family"

Allow:

* Add Mother
* Add Father
* Add Children

Features:

* Scam alerts
* Family notifications
* Emergency help

This should be one of the main premium features.

GUARDIAN MODE

Guardian Protection must be ON by default.

Enable:

* Scam Call Screening
* Scam SMS Screening
* Fraud Alert Monitoring

On first launch explain:

"Netraksh watches for scam calls and fraud messages to help keep you safe."

ON-DEVICE PROTECTION

Enable by default:

* Scam Call Detection
* Scam SMS Detection
* Suspicious Link Detection

Processing should happen on-device whenever possible.

MINIMIZE USER EFFORT

Users should never need to:

* Type long text
* Understand cybersecurity terms
* Navigate deep menus

Maximum actions:

* Tap
* Speak
* Share

TRUST DESIGN

Every screen should answer:

1. Is it safe?
2. What should I do next?

Examples:

Safe:
"You can continue."

Be Careful:
"Verify before sending money."

Dangerous:
"Do not click. This may be a scam."

USER EXPERIENCE RULE

A first-time smartphone user should understand every screen within 10 seconds.

If a feature requires explanation, redesign it until it becomes obvious.

SUCCESS METRIC

The app should be usable by:

* Senior citizens
* Rural users
* Non-English speakers
* First-time smartphone users

without training or technical knowledge.

FINAL POSITIONING

NETRAKSH

India's Digital Bodyguard

Protecting Every Indian from Scam Calls, Fraud Messages, Fake Links, QR Scams, and Online Fraud.
