Act as a Senior Mobile Architect, DevSecOps Engineer, QA Lead, Product Owner, SOC Architect, and Production Release Manager.

Perform a complete end-to-end audit of the Netraksh application and admin portal and prepare it for production launch.

OBJECTIVES

1. Remove all demo, test, placeholder, mock, and sample data.
2. Ensure every feature is fully functional and production-ready.
3. Validate all integrations.
